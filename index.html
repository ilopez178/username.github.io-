About me

PM by day.
Consultant by night.

I'm a product manager at Ramsey Solutions in Tennessee, leading product work across a three-sided real estate marketplace — agents, customers, and the business.

Over 5+ years I've shipped across the full funnel — SEO, paid acquisition, ML-powered connections, and AI-driven features. The focus is always the same: find the real problem, and use tech to solve it in a way that creates business value.

Clear communication is something I genuinely care about. I translate between engineers, executives, and customers — good product work lives or dies on that bridge.

On the side, I bring that same thinking to small businesses — monday.com setups, automations, and integrations built around how they actually work.
